## 2024-05-18 - [Interactive State Feedback & Accessible Labels]
**Learning:** Adding interactive state feedback (disabled states with clear disabled styles) and accessible labels (like `aria-label` and `htmlFor`) significantly improves keyboard navigation, form completion ease and screen reader support for game UIs, which otherwise often miss these essentials.
**Action:** Always ensure that custom action buttons (like game cards) provide appropriate `aria-label` texts indicating their action, and forms disable submission correctly and legibly prior to valid input.
